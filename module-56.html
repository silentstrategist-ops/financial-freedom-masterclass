The First
Message

The first message earns the reconnection. The ask comes later, once real warmth has been re-established.

Separating Reconnection From The Pitch

Trying to combine a reconnection and a pitch into a single message weakens both. The reconnection feels fake, and the pitch feels rushed.

The Outreach Deconstructor

Hover over the highlighted sections to see the mechanics of a perfect first message.

"Hey Sarah, it's been a while since we worked on that messy Q3 logistics rollout. I've been thinking about you lately and wanted to reconnect. I've recently moved into coaching mid-career ops managers, and I'd genuinely love to hear how things have been going on your end too. Would you have 15-20 minutes sometime in the next couple weeks for a quick call, no agenda, just good to catch up?"

Step 55 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01Pick one real person from your network and fill in the template with actual details from your history.
Question 02Read your filled-in message back. Does it sound like you, or does it sound like a template?
Question 03What's the actual worst realistic outcome if you send this message? Could you live with it?
Question 04Think of a "no agenda" message you've received. How did it make you feel compared to a disguised pitch?
Question 05Pick three people from your list and commit to sending this message to one of them this week. Who and when?
Question 06For each of those three, is the relationship casual or formal? How should that change the tone?

SEAL YOUR COMMITMENT

"I will reach out with zero agenda. I will focus entirely on genuine reconnection, letting the relationship exist as the asset it is."

TYPE "I ACCEPT" AND PRESS SEAL
