The Myth That's
Been Sold to You

Real innovation belongs to the young? The data says the opposite. Business success in the real world is about execution, risk management, and handling complicated situations with real people.

The 45.0 Reality

Among the fastest-growing 0.1% of new businesses, the average age of the founder is 45, not 25. A fifty-year-old founder is about 1.8 times more likely to build a runaway success than a thirty-year-old.
