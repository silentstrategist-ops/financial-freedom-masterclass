Choosing Your

Calling yourself a general life coach feels safe because it doesn't rule anyone out. In practice, a potential client has no clear reason to choose you.

Where A Real Lane Comes From

Your strongest lane isn't invented from scratch. It sits inside your Skills Audit and your own lived experience of change, struggle, and hard transition.

The Niche Narrower

Click to step away from the generic and toward the specific.

"Life Coach"
Too broad. Competing with millions. Means nothing.
"Business Coach"
Better, but still generic. Which business? What problem?
"Transition Coach for Mid-Career Managers"
"I help corporate managers plan a transition into running their own business without burning their safety net."
Step 48 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01What specific kind of change have you personally gone through, and genuinely come out the other side of?
Question 02What do people already, unprompted, come to you for advice about?
Question 03What professional world do you understand fluently from the inside?
Question 04Draft your own narrow lane in one clear sentence, naming exactly who it's for and what changes.
Question 05Does your sentence sound like it could describe anyone, or one very specific kind of person?
Question 06Who is one real person in your life right now who fits that exact description?
Question 07What fear do you have about narrowing your focus? Is it based on real evidence?
