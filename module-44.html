Financial Freedom Masterclass · Module 44

Translating the
Resume

A standard resume says what you were responsible for. A consulting offer says what changed because of you.

The Translation Shift

Nothing is invented. The underlying work is identical. What changes is the frame, moving from "what I was assigned to do" to "what actually changed because I was involved."

The Resume Translator

Teacher (15 Years)
"Taught secondary school mathematics and managed classroom behaviour."
Consulting Translation
"Designed structured curriculum that measurably improved performance across 30 students, managing complex dynamics under time pressure."
Accountant (20 Years)
"Prepared monthly financial reports and managed the bookkeeping process."
Consulting Translation
"Built and maintained the full financial reporting system for a mid-sized operation, identifying and correcting costly errors before leadership."
Salesperson (12 Years)
"Responsible for client outreach and closing new accounts."
Consulting Translation
"Built a repeatable outreach process that consistently converted cold leads into long-term accounts in a highly difficult market."
Step 43 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01Choose one real achievement from your own career and rewrite it using the action, scope, result shape.
Question 02Read your new version back out loud. Does it sound like something a stranger would genuinely pay for, or does it still sound like a job description?
Question 03Looking at the three worked examples (Teacher, Accountant, Sales), which one is closest to your own field?
Question 04List three specific, recurring problems in your industry that you already know how to solve.
Question 05Who are three people from your past working life you could reasonably reach out to first, simply to reconnect honestly?
Question 06Draft a short message to one of them that reconnects first, referencing something real you shared, without asking for anything yet.
Question 07What has actually been stopping you from sending a message like that until now?

SEAL YOUR COMMITMENT

"I will stop listing my responsibilities and start communicating my outcomes. I sell specific, provable changes."
