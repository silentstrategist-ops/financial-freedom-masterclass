Financial Freedom Masterclass · Module 33

The
Private Record

Your operating manual for your own value. It is the source of truth about what you actually do, what you produce, and what you are worth.

Not for Public Consumption

This is not your LinkedIn profile. It is the document you open before a negotiation, before a discovery call, before writing a proposal. It prevents you from having to "remember" your achievements under pressure.

The Living Document Structure

Click to acknowledge the required sections of your Private Record.

✓

A. The One-Line Value Statement

One sentence. Updated quarterly as your focus sharpens.

✓

B. The CCRI Database

12-15 records spanning 5 years. Your proof-of-work archive.

✓

C. The Shadow Work Inventory

Your tacit-knowledge contributions outside formal job descriptions.

✓

D. The Floor-Price Calculation

Your minimum viable rate, recalculated annually.

✓

E. The Tacit Knowledge Map

Your deepest domain of pattern recognition and translation.

✓

F. The "Market Signal" Log

A running list of every time someone asks you for advice.
