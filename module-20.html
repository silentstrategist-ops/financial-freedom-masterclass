The Financial World
You Already Know

SACCOs, groups, table banking, and mobile money are not a backup plan. They are trusted tools that quietly build real wealth for millions.

This chapter treats local tools with the seriousness they deserve, while being honest about where the real risks sit. They work brilliantly—when governed by strict rules, not just blind trust.

🤝
SACCOs
Builds steady saving discipline and gives access to credit based on multiplier rules, better than banks.
CHECK: Is it properly registered? Does it publish clear financial reports? Can members easily exit?
👥
Informal Groups
Creates real accountability. It allows people to pool resources for bigger investments (land/business).
CHECK: Are rules written down? Are records fully transparent to everyone, not just one leader?
🪑
Table Banking
Simple, local, transparent. Members borrow directly from the pot, and the interest benefits the group.
CHECK: Everyone must be in the room. Clear limits on borrowing ratios relative to contributions.
📱
Mobile Money Savings
Small, steady amounts directly from your phone. Easiest way to build the early stages of a liquidity buffer.
CHECK: How fast can you actually withdraw in an emergency? Convenience in must match convenience out.

The Risk Worth Naming: Currency Risk

If your local currency regularly loses value against stronger currencies, your savings quietly lose buying power even as the number grows. This is why you must eventually spread some wealth into international assets (like broad ETFs) to protect your real purchasing power.
