Financial Freedom Masterclass · Module 24

Why This Module
Isn't Optional

Money habits are rarely solitary. They're absorbed, negotiated, and hidden inside a household. The health of your finances is inseparable from the health of your relationships.

🗣️
Talking to a Partner
Financial secrecy is incredibly common. The fix isn't one dramatic conversation. It's a recurring, low-stakes check-in where both people can see the same numbers and name the same goals before resentments calcify.
~40% of adults in committed relationships admit to keeping a financial secret.
🌱
Teaching Children
Parents are consistently the single largest influence on children's financial attitudes, transmitted through observation. They notice if money is discussed calmly or anxiously long before they understand the math.
Basic cognitive "habits of mind" for money are largely formed by age 7.
📜
The Basics of a Will
A will doesn't need to be perfect. It just needs to name who receives what, who cares for dependents, and who executes it. Don't forget beneficiary designations on retirement accounts—they override the will.
Only 24% of American adults currently have a will.
Step 23 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01How often does money actually get discussed openly in your household, and who tends to avoid the topic?
Question 02Is there anything financial you've never fully disclosed to your partner? What would it take to change that?
Question 03What did you personally absorb about money from watching your own parents, and what are you passing on to your children?
Question 04If you have children, what is one real, small financial decision you could let them make this year?
Question 05Do you currently have a will? If not, what is the actual, honest reason you've put it off?
Question 06If something happened to you tomorrow, would the people closest to you actually know where to find your accounts and passwords?
Question 07Are your beneficiary designations on retirement and insurance accounts current, or have they gone stale?
Question 08Who would you want making decisions on your behalf if you couldn't, and have you ever told them directly?
Question 09What's one specific money conversation you've been avoiding having with someone in your life?
Question 10Write down the exact date you'll have that conversation, or take that estate-planning step, by. A date, not "soon."
