The Math & Your Psychology
Are Not The Same Problem.

Most debt advice picks a side and argues it like there's one correct answer. There isn't. The right method depends entirely on which failure mode is more likely to actually happen to you.

Clear The Leak Before You Fill The Bucket

High interest debt is a negative compounding force. Clearing a 24% debt is mathematically equivalent to earning a guaranteed 24% return, risk-free. Almost no legitimate investment offers that.

Don't skip building this list properly to jump to the method. The method is meaningless without accurate numbers underneath it.

The Honest Question
When you've tried structured financial plans before, why did you abandon them?
"I gave up because progress felt too slow." I need fast, visible wins to stay motivated, even if it mathematically costs slightly more interest.
"I stuck with it because I trusted the logic." I can survive a long stretch without a visible win, as long as I know I am saving the absolute maximum amount of money.

Visualising The Rollover Effect

Follow the arrows below. When Debt 1 is cleared, its $1,500 minimum payment doesn't get absorbed into your lifestyle. It rolls directly into the next debt on your list, creating a $4,500 payment. By the time the final debt is cleared, you are left with a massive $10,500 monthly surplus.

Whichever method you choose, the discipline of the monthly payment does not disappear when the debt does. It just changes jobs.
