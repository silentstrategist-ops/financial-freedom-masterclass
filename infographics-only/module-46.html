Escaping
Hourly Pricing

The better and faster you get at solving a problem, the less you earn. This is precisely backwards from how real expertise should be rewarded.

The Pricing Evolution

Consultants evolve through pricing stages: Hourly → Deliverables-Based → Tiered Packages → Value-Based → Performance-Based. But before you can evolve, you must know your survival floor.

The Profit Floor Calculator

Formula: Monthly Cost / (1 - Target Margin Decimal). If Cost = 2000 and Margin = 40% (0.40), Floor = 2000 / 0.60 = 3333.

Pricing below this floor actively punishes you for taking on clients, because more work simply means losing money faster.

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01If you were consulting today, which of the five pricing models would fit your first offer best, and why?
Question 02Roughly add up your true monthly cost to properly serve one client (time, tools, overhead). Write your honest estimate.
Question 03Using the floor price formula (Cost / (1 - Target Margin)), what is the lowest amount you could charge without losing money?
Question 04Have you ever priced something based on fear of losing the client rather than on what the work genuinely required? What happened?
Question 05As your track record grows, which pricing model would you want to move toward?
Question 06Think of a specific result you've delivered. How would you price that outcome if you were pricing the result instead of the hours?
