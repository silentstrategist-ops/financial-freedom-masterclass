Visibility Is A Skill

The Executive Translation

There is a meaningful difference between exaggerating and simply making your real contribution known. Visibility isn't about talking the loudest. It's about a habit.

Whenever you complete something meaningful, write one sentence in the language decision-makers actually respond to: A clear action, a specific result, and a number. Do not wait for your review to scramble for memories.

Negotiation Is An Exchange Of Information

Say It Plainly, Then Let The Silence Sit.

Most pay decisions are not made maliciously. They're made with incomplete information by people juggling budgets. A negotiation is simply you supplying information the decision-maker didn't fully have.

Most people undercut their own ask by immediately softening it with an apology or a justification the moment they've said it. The discomfort of that silence is not a sign you did something wrong.
