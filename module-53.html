When someone already trusts the person who introduced you, a huge chunk of the work is already done before you've said a single word.

The Transfer of Trust

A referral isn't just a name passed along. The person making the introduction is quietly putting a small amount of their own credibility on the line. That risk is exactly why a referral carries so much weight.

10% Trust

Step 52 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01List five people from your past working life who you haven't spoken to in over a year. What's one honest reason you haven't reached out?
Question 02Think of a time someone was personally referred to you. How differently did you treat that conversation compared to a cold pitch?
Question 03Have you ever "spent" your own trust by referring someone to a friend? What made you comfortable doing that?
Question 04Which of the five people from question one would you feel most comfortable reaching out to first, with no agenda?
Question 05What's one thing you're currently waiting to "have" before you reach back out? Is that wait necessary or avoidance?
Question 06Think honestly about your career. How many real, working relationships did you build without realizing they were an asset? Name three.

SEAL YOUR COMMITMENT

"I will stop waiting until I "have something to offer." Reconnecting now keeps trust warm; waiting turns it transactional."
