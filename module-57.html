Tracking Without
Being Creepy

The line isn't about whether you keep notes. It's about the intent behind them. Are you tracking to show up better, or tracking to extract?

The Minimalist Tracking System

You don't need CRM software. You need 5 columns: Name, Context, Last Contact, Discussed, Next Follow-up. The goal isn't complex tagging; it's simply not letting real relationships slip through the cracks.

The Care vs. Management Test

Test your intent. Paste a CRM note below to test if it passes the "Would they wince?" test.

Step 56 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01Do you currently have any system for tracking who's in your network? What does it look like right now?
Question 02Sketch out what a simple tracking sheet (Name, Context, Last Contact, Discussed, Next Follow-Up) for your top ten contacts would look like.
Question 03If someone saw your notes about them, would they feel cared for or managed? Be honest with yourself.
Question 04What's a realistic check-in rhythm for you personally given your actual schedule and energy?
Question 05Pick three people from your network right now and write down when you last actually spoke to each of them.
Question 06Think of one specific, real detail about someone in your life that you'd genuinely want to remember to ask about next time.
