Financial Freedom Masterclass · Module 51

Earning Trust
Before The Sale

A potential client is evaluating whether they are willing to be genuinely honest with you about things they haven't said out loud yet.

The Discovery Call

This isn't a sales pitch dressed up as a favour. It's a low-pressure conversation where the potential client gets an honest taste of what working with you actually feels like.

1
Ask & Listen
Ask genuine questions about their real situation and listen without waiting to pitch.
2
Reflect
Reflect back what you're hearing so they feel truly understood, not just heard.
3
Determine Fit
If it's a fit, explain how you help. If it isn't, say so honestly and walk away.
Step 50 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01If you ran a discovery call today, what three or four genuine questions would you actually ask?
Question 02Think of a time you were sold something with pressure vs honesty. Which made you trust them more?
Question 03Who are two or three people who might be willing to be an early client in exchange for honest feedback?
Question 04If you asked a satisfied client directly for a referral, what would you say in your own words?
Question 05Write one honest sentence describing who your coaching is genuinely for.
Question 06What real, honest outcome would you want a future testimonial to actually describe?

SEAL YOUR COMMITMENT

"I will not use pressure tactics. I will earn trust through genuine discovery, clear communication, and honest boundaries."
