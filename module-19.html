The Familiar Route
To Wealth

For a lot of people, land and property feel more real than a fund on a screen. But getting this wrong can be very costly. Let's treat it honestly.

The Part People Often Get Wrong

Property is not spread out. It's a single, concentrated bet on one specific place. Property is not liquid. You cannot sell a plot of land overnight to cover an emergency.

More people lose money in land through unclear ownership, fake deeds, and boundary disputes than through the land itself losing value.

The Buyer's Checklist

Work through this fully before any money changes hands, every time, even if the seller is someone you trust. Click to check off.

1. Official Title Search

Confirm the title deed is genuine through an official government search, not just by looking at the paper the seller hands you.

2. Consent of All Owners

Confirm the seller is the legal owner and there are no other names on the title that haven't given their formal consent.

3. Physical Boundary Verification

Physically visit the land with a licensed surveyor to confirm the boundaries match the official records.

4. Debt & Dispute Check

Check whether there are any existing debts, disputes, or claims tied to that specific piece of land or property.

5. Legal Transfer

Use a qualified lawyer to handle the transfer properly, rather than trying to save a small fee informally.
