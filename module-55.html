Engineering
Referrals

Most people have every intention of helping, right up until the moment they'd need to actually stop, think, and act on it. Your job is to make that moment easy.

The Specific Ask

"Let me know if you hear of anyone" is the referral equivalent of "we should catch up sometime." It gives them nothing to act on. A specific ask works: "Is there anyone in your world right now going through something similar to what you were dealing with?"

The Referral Timing Scale

Timing matters more than most people realize.

The Breakthrough
High Leverage. Ask right after a win.
Unprompted Praise
Strong Opening. They brought up the value naturally.
6 Months Later
Weakest. The specific feeling has cooled into vague goodwill.
Step 54 Integration

Plan Your Own Approach

Question 01Think of a past client where you delivered real value. Did you ever directly ask them if they knew anyone else? Why or why not?
Question 02Write out exactly what you'd say to that person if you reached out today using the specific-ask format.
Question 03What's the actual fear underneath your hesitation to ask directly? Is it based on a real past experience?
Question 04Think of the last time you referred someone else's business. What specifically made that easy for you to do?
Question 05If someone told you "no one comes to mind right now," how would you respond to keep the door open without pressure?
Question 06Write one or two sentences describing specifically who you're looking to help.

SEAL YOUR COMMITMENT

"I will stop waiting for referrals to magically appear. I will gently, specifically, and calmly ask for them at the right moment."
